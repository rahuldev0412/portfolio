iPhone

Install with TestFlight

TestFlight is Apple's free app for trying apps before they reach the App Store. It takes about two minutes.

  1. 1

    Get the TestFlight app

    On your iPhone, open the App Store, search for TestFlight and tap Get. It is made by Apple and is free.

    open_in_new Open TestFlight in App Store
  2. 2

    Tap the invite link

    Come back to this page on your iPhone and tap the button below. It opens TestFlight with Upstat ready to go.

    rocket_launch Join the Upstat beta
  3. 3

    Accept and install

    In TestFlight, tap Accept, then Install. Upstat appears on your home screen like any other app.

  4. 4

    Stay up to date

    New versions show up in TestFlight. Tap Update when you see one. To send feedback, take a screenshot while using Upstat and tap Share Beta Feedback.

Something not working?
  • "This beta is full" or "not accepting testers": the tester limit was reached. Reach out and I'll make room.
  • The link opens a web page instead of TestFlight: install TestFlight first (step 1), then tap the link again.
  • "Requires a newer iOS": update your iPhone in Settings > General > Software Update.
  • The app stopped opening: beta builds expire after 90 days. Open TestFlight and install the latest version.
Android

Install the APK

An APK is the install file for an Android app. Download it on your phone and open it to install.

  1. 1

    Download the file

    Tap the button below on your Android phone.

    download Download APK
  2. 2

    Open it

    When the download finishes, tap Open in the notification, or find upstat.apk in your Files or Downloads app.

  3. 3

    Allow the install

    Android may say apps from this source aren't allowed. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

  4. 4

    Tap Install

    If Google Play Protect warns that the app is unrecognized, tap More details and then Install anyway. This shows for any app not downloaded from the Play Store.
